OB-12 Audio Unit

A hardware-level emulation of the Oberheim OB-12: the original Master and Slave firmware run on emulated F2MC-16 CPUs and TMS57070 DSPs.

Installs OB12.component into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (Apple Silicon and Intel). In Logic Pro: AU Instruments → Tuth → OB-12.

The instrument needs about 12 seconds after loading before the first note sounds: the Slave boots as on the unit. The unit's memory (user programs, settings) is kept in ~/Library/Application Support/OB12.
